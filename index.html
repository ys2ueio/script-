Features

Built feature by feature,
tested release after release

Moon Hub focuses on a small set of tools that are genuinely tuned — no filler toggles. Each one is refined until it behaves the same way every single run.

01 Combat & Gameplay

Fix Speed

Smooth, stable movement driven by a proxy system. Motion is pushed every Stepped frame through proxyMove, and the real velocity comes from AssemblyLinearVelocity applied to a welded proxy part — not from rewriting the humanoid speed over and over. Server-side WalkSpeed stays forced at 34.

  • proxyMove
  • Stepped
  • AssemblyLinearVelocity
  • WalkSpeed 34

Bat Counter

An automatic counter built around the game's combat system and wired into the ragdoll system, so reactions stay consistent instead of depending on your timing.

  • BatCounter
  • Ragdoll-aware

Auto Play Mode

Three levels of automation so you decide how much the hub does for you — from fully hands-off to a light assist while you stay in control.

  • Full
  • Half
  • Semi

Irish Auto Steal

An optimised auto steal that stays synchronised with the game server: plot data is requested once, then kept live through server updates. A client-side READY / UNREADY indicator tells you the exact state before anything fires, and hold timings are randomised inside a tuned window rather than fixed.

  • Steal range 8 studs
  • Prime range 70 studs
  • Hold 1.3–2.6 s
  • Cooldown 0.05 s

READY live status, straight in the UI

02 Visual & Comfort

Freeze Effects

Strips the superfluous graphical layers — gradients, strokes and halos — for a cleaner, sharper render that can also be lighter on your device.

  • Gradients
  • Strokes
  • Halos

Lag Reduction

A dedicated pass that takes weight off your device: unnecessary visual effects are cut back, the scene has less to draw, and the render stays lighter. Less graphical load means a smoother, steadier session — more of your device's budget goes to actually playing.

  • Lighter render
  • Less graphical load
  • Steadier feel

Stability first

Heavy startup work is offloaded so the interface builds without stalling your client, and the script is structured to stay inside Luau's limits. Boring on purpose — a hub that loads the same way every time.

  • Non-blocking startup
  • Luau-safe structure

03 Utility

Speed Bypass

An advanced speed bypass with its own widget and a power slider, so you dial in exactly the amount you need. Bound to the M key for instant access.

  • Power slider
  • Shortcut M

Lagger

A network disruption tool with four calibrated levels, so you can go from a light touch to full intensity without editing anything.

  • Low
  • Mid
  • High
  • Ultra
